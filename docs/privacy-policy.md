# PumaRun Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

PumaRun is a GPS running app. This policy explains what the app accesses, how it is
used, and where it is stored.

## Location

PumaRun requests precise device location while you use the running features. Location
is used to show your current position, measure distance and pace, draw your route,
and save a completed run on the device.

PumaRun does not upload your routes to a PumaRun server. The app has no accounts,
advertising, analytics, or social features.

The app can continue collecting location while a run is active and the screen is
off. Android displays a foreground-service notification during an active run. You
can stop the run from the app or its notification and can revoke location access in
Android settings.

## Map data

PumaRun loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Requests for map tiles can reveal the
approximate map area being viewed to the map tile provider. OpenStreetMap data is
available under the [Open Database License](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
The required OpenStreetMap attribution is shown on every map.

## Saved runs

Completed runs, including their route points and summary statistics, are stored in
the app's private local storage. They remain on the device until you delete them
from the Tracks screen or uninstall PumaRun. PumaRun does not sell or share these
saved routes with third parties.

## Text-to-speech

Voice announcements are handled by the text-to-speech engine selected on the device.
Depending on that engine, speech processing may be local or provided by the engine's
vendor. PumaRun does not send voice recordings because it does not record audio.

## Permissions

PumaRun may request:

- Location, to measure and display a run.
- Notifications, to show the active-run notification on supported Android versions.
- Internet access, to load map tiles.
- Wake lock access, to keep an active run measuring while the screen is off.

## Data deletion and contact

You can delete individual saved runs from Tracks. Uninstalling PumaRun removes its
local app data. PumaRun does not create user accounts.

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the PumaRun Google Play listing
or the project repository:
<https://github.com/wecalderonc/PumaRun>
